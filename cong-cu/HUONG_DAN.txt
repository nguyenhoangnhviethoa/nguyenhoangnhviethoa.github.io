CONG CU CHUAN BI DANG BAN VIET HOA (NH Viet Hoa)

Mo: nhap dup "Dang ban moi.bat" (lan dau tu cai thu vien anh Pillow).

1. Chon thu muc ban dich (vd Downloads\VietHoa_BoCai\Shogun2) -> bam "Doc thong tin".
2. Xem muc 2: 3 cho so phien ban (ten zip, THONG_TIN_WEB.md, ma nguon) co dau tich xanh la khop.
   Lech nhau thi cong cu lay so lon nhat va canh bao mau vang -> kiem tra lai truoc khi ghi.
3. Muc 3: chon game (tu nhan ra theo ten) hoac "+ Game moi" (dien ma id va repo).
   Phien ban game: ghi theo ngay upload. Chon anh bia (anh menu co logo) / anh trong game neu co.
   "Lay lai noi dung trang tu THONG_TIN_WEB.md": chi tick khi muon thay gioi thieu, da dich,
   gioi han, huong dan cai bang noi dung moi trong file md.
4. Bam "Xem truoc thay doi" -> doc phan xanh/do -> bam "Ghi vao thu muc web".
   Ban cu cua games.js luon duoc giu o games.js.bak.

Sau khi ghi: nho Claude "day ban moi len web" (hoac tu tao Release + day games.js va anh moi).
Ghi chu phat hanh soan san nam trong thu muc "_ban-phat-hanh (khong upload len web)", file .release.md.

KHONG day len GitHub: games.js.bak, thu muc _ban-phat-hanh.
